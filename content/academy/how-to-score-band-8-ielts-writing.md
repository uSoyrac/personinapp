# How to Score Band 8 in IELTS Writing Task 2

Reaching Band 8 in IELTS Writing Task 2 is not about using rare, impressive words. It is about answering the exact question, organising your ideas clearly, and controlling your grammar and vocabulary with precision. The examiner scores you on four equally weighted criteria, and a Band 8 essay performs strongly on all of them. This guide breaks down what each one rewards and how to hit it consistently.

## Understand the four marking criteria

Every Task 2 essay is graded on Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy. A common reason capable writers stall at Band 6.5 is that they pour energy into vocabulary while ignoring Task Response. Treat all four as equal — your final band is the average.

## Answer the whole question

Band 8 essays directly address every part of the prompt. If the question asks whether the advantages outweigh the disadvantages, you must take a clear position and defend it, not simply list both sides. Underline the task keywords, decide your position before you write, and make sure your introduction states it in one sentence.

## Use a clear, repeatable structure

You do not need a creative structure — you need a clear one:

- **Introduction:** paraphrase the question and state your position.
- **Body paragraph 1:** one main idea, explained and supported with a specific example.
- **Body paragraph 2:** a second main idea, developed the same way.
- **Conclusion:** restate your position; add no new ideas.

One developed idea per paragraph beats three shallow ones. Examiners reward depth, not quantity.

## Build cohesion that feels natural

Cohesion is the invisible glue between sentences. Overusing linkers such as "Moreover" and "Furthermore" actually lowers your score because it feels mechanical. Instead, use referencing ("this trend", "such measures") and a few well-placed connectors. Each paragraph should open with a clear topic sentence that signals its single main idea.

## Show lexical precision, not just big words

Band 8 vocabulary is precise and natural. Instead of forcing "plethora" or "myriad", use exact collocations: *address a problem*, *a significant impact*, *widely regarded as*. Save unfamiliar words you meet while reading into your dictionary and review them in context — that is how active vocabulary grows.

## Control your grammar

Aim for a mix of simple, compound, and complex sentences with very few errors. A Band 8 writer makes occasional slips but never lets them blur meaning. If complex sentences cause mistakes, prioritise accuracy: a clear correct sentence scores higher than an ambitious broken one.

## Common mistakes that cap your score

- Memorised templates that do not fit the specific question.
- Listing ideas without developing any of them.
- Writing under 250 words (an automatic penalty).
- Spending so long on Task 2 that Task 1 suffers — budget 40 minutes.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long should a Task 2 essay be?**
Aim for 260–290 words. Long enough to develop two ideas fully, short enough to stay accurate.

**Do I need academic examples?**
No. Specific, realistic examples — even from everyday life — are more persuasive than vague academic claims.

**How do I practise effectively?**
Write under timed conditions, then compare your essay against the band descriptors and rewrite the weakest paragraph.
